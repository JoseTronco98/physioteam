  • Problem: a one-room practice is also its own receptionist, biller and secretary: about 130 minutes a day of WhatsApp, forms and payments in the case-study clinic, mostly at 8:00 and after closing
  • Solution: six agents and 58 tools run booking, policy, waitlist, payments, every clinical form and doctor report, on the clinic's templates

  • Evidence: Case study of a solo physical therapy clinic in Mexico (source: Juliana del Bosque, PT; initial estimate, n = 1): about 111 minutes a day of admin freed, and about MXN 8,800 a month in impact from the clinic's 8:00 hour.

Solo physical therapists lose a working day a week to WhatsApp and paperwork, and empty slots to late cancellations

  • ~25 patients × 3 sessions a week, all coordinated on the therapist's personal WhatsApp
  • Prices, "can I come Thursday?", reminders, cancellations, "how much do I owe?", invoices
  • Then a 7-section evaluation, a SOAP note per session, plans, doctor reports
  • ~70% of PT patients don't finish their plan (WebPT); every empty hour is MXN 400-600
  • ~78 M WhatsApp users in Mexico: the channel patients already use
The physical therapist who tested it: the three pains are booking over WhatsApp ("maybe two minutes per patient"), diagnosis and follow-up reports for referring doctors, and finances (who has paid, who owes the clinic): about 130 minutes a day by the tester's best estimate.
Note: volumes measured in the simulated clinic week; minutes per task as in report Exhibit 20; the tester's best estimate is ~130 min a day. Source: PhysioTeam simulation; user test; WebPT; DPL News.
